## Landing Page Architecture

`src/pages/index.astro` composes the one-page site from section components, in this order: Hero → PainPoints → Method → HowITeach → LessonsDifferent → Services → Results → Testimonials → About → Contact. About sits just before Contact by design (it reads better as a "who's behind this" beat right before the CTA, rather than as a second impression right after Hero). Testimonials renders nothing (no `<section>` at all) when `content.testimonials.items` is empty, so the section can be emptied out via the admin editor without leaving a gap in the page or the nav.

Sections no longer show a small "eyebrow" pill above their `<h2>` — `Services.astro`, `Testimonials.astro`, `About.astro`, `Contact.astro`, and now `Hero.astro` each render only their real heading now (`HeroContent.eyebrow` was removed from the type/JSON/admin editor along with it — it was already empty and had no slot in the redesigned Hero). `SectionKicker.astro` still exists as a component but nothing imports it; it was kept in case the pattern is wanted again rather than deleted outright.

`Method.astro`, `HowITeach.astro`, and `LessonsDifferent.astro` are **three separate top-level sections** (`Method.astro` matches `Ideas/Mockups/Method.jpg`; the other two match `Ideas/Mockups/Method2.jpg`) — not, as an earlier iteration on this branch had it, "teach"/"timeline" bands stacked inside one `<section id="method">`. That first attempt nested the two bands inside Method's own padded section, which visually read as "inside the My Method background" rather than truly full-width like Hero/About; being full-bleed *CSS* (a 2-column grid not wrapped in `.container`) wasn't enough while they were still descendants of a padded parent section. The fix was structural, not CSS: each band became its own top-level `<section>` (`how-i-teach`, `lessons-different`), rendered as a sibling of `Method` in `index.astro`, with its own content-model interface, its own `visible` toggle, and its own admin fieldset — matching how every other section already works. **If you find a doc or memory describing "part 1"/"part 2" bands inside one Method section, it's stale — don't resurrect that structure without discussing it first.** Neither new section was added to `Navbar.astro`'s 6-link nav — same precedent as `pain-points` (see "Navigation" below for the current link list, which has since swapped `results` for `testimonials`): they render and are individually toggleable, but don't get a nav link or scroll-spy entry.

**`Method.astro`** (branch `redo-method-polaroid-grid`) dropped the old 2-column-top-plus-6-card-grid layout entirely — **if you find a doc or memory describing `.method-top`/`.method-copy`/`.method-portrait`/a z-index+negative-margin overlap with a 6-card `.method-grid` here, it's stale**, that structure is gone, not just restyled. The section is now: a centered `<h2 class="method-title">` (same `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Method"]` highlight list, `--font-display`, uppercase — just centered now instead of left-aligned inside a 2-column row) followed by `.method-stage`, a `display:grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(2,1fr); grid-template-rows: repeat(2,1fr)` 2×2 grid holding exactly 4 `.method-card.polaroid` cards (down from 6 — there is no photo/copy row above it anymore, and no overlap trick). A `.method-center-text` sits `position:absolute; top/left:50%; translate:-50% -50%` over the grid's middle gap, showing `content.introSubtitle` with `background: var(--color-secondary-pale)` behind it (matching the section's own background) so it visually blends into the gap instead of needing its own card/frame. Collapses to a single column at ≤800px, where `.method-center-text` drops out of absolute positioning (`position:static`) and stacks below the cards instead.
- Each `.method-card.polaroid` reuses the `.polaroid` pattern (white background, `padding: 0.75rem 0.75rem 2.25rem`, `box-shadow: var(--shadow-lift)`) already established by `PainPoints.astro` and `About.astro` — this is now the **third** usage; see "No more polaroid photo treatment" below, whose framing has been updated accordingly. Method's own usage adds one wrinkle the other two don't have: alternating tilt (`rotate: -3deg` on odd cards, `rotate: 3deg` on even cards via `.method-card:nth-child(2n)`), rather than a single fixed rotation. Each card shows `item.image` (`PlaceholderImage variant="square"`) as the photo plus a new `.method-card-caption` (`item.title` only, bold, centered) in the polaroid's bottom band. `item.description` is deliberately **not** rendered by this component — it stays in the content model and admin editor, unused here, not deleted (unlike `image`/`introText` below, which were actually removed).
- **`MethodContent` (`src/types/content.ts`) is now `{ title, introSubtitle, items, visible }`** — `image` (the old standalone circular portrait) and `introText` (the old longer paragraph) were both dropped outright, not just unrendered. `introSubtitle` is the same field as before but repurposed: it used to sit as top-copy alongside the title, now it's the `.method-center-text` shown in the grid's middle gap. `content/siteContent.json`'s `method.items` was trimmed from 6 to 4 entries to match the 2×2 grid, and its `method.image` key was removed entirely. The admin's "Method Section" fieldset lost its image-upload field and the `introText` textarea accordingly (see the admin-editor paragraph above, which predates this pass and is now partly superseded by it).

**`HowITeach.astro`** (`<section id="how-i-teach">`) — full-bleed 2-column grid (NOT wrapped in `.container`, same pattern as `Hero.astro`'s `.hero-inner`/`About.astro`'s `.about-inner`), now a genuine top-level sibling section with no ancestor section padding — this is what actually fixes the full-width issue, not the grid CSS alone. Left column: `content.image` via `PlaceholderImage variant="rounded"` — `HowITeachContent.image` is its **own** field (seeded `/images/lara_about.jpg`), **not** a reuse of `method.image` the way the earlier iteration did; every section now owns its content independently. Its right edge fades into the gradient panel next to it via `mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, black 55%, transparent 100%)` on `:global(.placeholder-image)` — explicitly mirroring Hero's own portrait-fade technique (`Hero` uses `to left` since its photo is on the right; this one uses `to right` since its photo is on the left). **The gradient background lives on the outer `.how-i-teach` section itself, not on `.how-i-teach-copy`** — Hero's exact gradient formula verbatim (`--color-primary` base + `linear-gradient(160deg, ...)` of `color-mix()`d primary/primary-dark) is set on the section, so masking the photo to transparent on the left reveals the *same* teal gradient underneath rather than blank space. (An earlier pass put the gradient on `.how-i-teach-copy` instead, which meant the photo's fade revealed whatever sat behind it — nothing — so it faded to white instead of blending into teal; moving the background up to the section fixed it and made this a genuine structural match to `Hero.astro`, whose gradient has likewise always lived on the outer `.hero` section, not a sub-column.) `.how-i-teach-copy` itself carries no background of its own — just white text, padded via a nested `.container`. Heading (`content.heading`) uses the same treatment as `Method.astro`'s title — `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)`, bumped up from an earlier smaller `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)` — with `"teach"` hardcoded as the `splitHighlighted()` highlight word (`var(--color-secondary)`). Body = `content.text`. `public/images/spritz_stamp.svg` sits bottom-right as a decorative white mask (same `mask-image` + `background-color: var(--color-white)` technique as `Hero.astro`'s `.hero-italy`), now tilted `rotate: 12deg` (previously untilted). `HowITeachContent`: `{ heading, text, image, visible }`.

**`LessonsDifferent.astro`** (`<section id="lessons-different">`, branch `redo-lessons-different-cards`) — solid `--color-primary` background (was `--color-white`), centered heading using the same Method-title treatment (`--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)`), `"different"` hardcoded as the `splitHighlighted()` highlight word. Heading base color is now `var(--color-white)` (was `--color-primary-dark`, no longer readable against the new solid-primary background) — the highlighted word keeps its own unchanged `var(--color-secondary)`. Below it, `content.steps.map(...)` (`LessonsStep = { title, description }` — same shape as the removed `MethodStep`) renders a 4-column `.lessons-grid` of `.lessons-card`s: white background, `border-radius: var(--radius-md)`, `box-shadow: var(--shadow-soft)`, generous padding, `min-height: 22rem` — the same "white card on a saturated section background" precedent Testimonials/Results already established, not a new idiom introduced here. **The entire earlier horizontal-timeline UI is gone** — the dashed connector line (`.timeline::before`), numbered circles (`.timeline-circle`), vertical dashed ticks (`.timeline-connector`), and outline SVG icons (`.timeline-icon`) have all been removed. **If you find a doc or memory describing a `.timeline`/`.timeline-circle`/`.timeline-connector`/`.timeline-icon` structure here, it's stale — don't resurrect it without discussing first.** Each card is now just three stacked elements, top to bottom: `.lessons-card-number` (plain text `"01"`–`"04"`, no icon — "no icons" was explicit feedback, a deliberate departure from every other icon-bearing card list on the site), `.lessons-card-title`, `.lessons-card-description`. The old `timelineColors` array (previously the circles'/icons' shared color) was renamed `cardAccents` and repurposed as the number badge's text color — same 4-value array (`--color-primary`, `--color-primary-dark`, `--color-secondary`, `--color-tertiary`), same positional-not-admin-editable convention, just a new name and a new job. The `italy_stamp.svg` decoration stays **top-left** (`left: 0; top: -1rem`, unchanged position) but is retinted `var(--color-white)` at lower opacity (`0.5`, down from `0.85`) since the old `var(--color-primary)` tint would now be invisible against the section's own new primary-colored background — the same "white watermark on a saturated background" technique `HowITeach.astro`'s `spritz_stamp` already uses. `LessonsDifferentContent`: `{ heading, steps: LessonsStep[], visible }` — unchanged, no content-model change from this pass.

### About: title merges greeting+name, mirrors HowITeach's gradient layout with columns reversed

`About.astro` was rebuilt (branch `redo-about`) to match `Ideas/Mockups/Aboutme.jpg`, reusing `HowITeach.astro`'s full-bleed dark-gradient pattern rather than inventing a new one. `AboutContent` (`{ greeting, name, bio, image, visible }`) is unchanged — pure component rewrite, no content-model change.

- **Title merges two existing fields into one heading; no new field was added.** Previously `content.greeting` rendered as a small separate italic `<p class="greeting">` above a separate `<h2>{content.name}</h2>`. Now both live in one `<h2 class="about-title">`: `<span class="highlight">{content.greeting}</span> {content.name}` — the same `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)` treatment as every other redone section's title. Base color `var(--color-white)`, the greeting span `var(--color-secondary)`.
- **Background moved from plain white onto the outer `.about` section**, using Hero's/HowITeach's gradient formula verbatim (`background-color: var(--color-primary)` + `background-image: linear-gradient(160deg, color-mix(...) 88% 0%, color-mix(...) 94% 100%)`). The old `.about-blob` decorative SVG (a low-opacity primary-colored blob, sized for the white background) was removed outright — it doesn't suit the dark gradient, and `HowITeach` (the section this now mirrors) has no equivalent blob.
- **Layout is `HowITeach`'s grid with columns reversed, and the `.about-inner` wrapper div that used to exist is gone.** `grid-template-columns: minmax(280px, 45%) 1fr` now lives directly on the outer `.about` section — no intermediate wrapper, exactly matching `HowITeach`'s structure (which also has none). Where `HowITeach` is photo-left/copy-right, `About` is copy-left (`.about-copy`, column 1) / photo-right (`.about-photo`, column 2).
- **Photo is a centered polaroid, not a full-height fading panel — this superseded an earlier pass on this same branch.** The original rebuild gave `About`'s photo (right column) a `to left` mask-image fade mirroring `HowITeach`'s `to right` fade (itself mirroring `Hero`'s), with `.about-photo` stretched to `height:100%` and `PlaceholderImage variant="rounded"` forced edge-to-edge via `width/height:100%; object-fit:cover; border-radius:0`. Branch `redo-about-polaroid` removed that fade entirely and replaced it: `.about-photo` no longer stretches (`display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center` instead), centering a new `.about-photo-frame.polaroid` wrapper around `PlaceholderImage` (variant changed to `"square"`). This reuses `PainPoints.astro`'s `.polaroid` treatment verbatim (white background, `padding: 0.75rem 0.75rem 2.25rem`, `box-shadow: var(--shadow-lift)`, `rotate: -3deg`, forced `aspect-ratio:1/1` crop) — see the polaroid-exception note in "No more polaroid photo treatment" below, now a second, still explicitly-scoped usage, not a general reintroduction of frames. **If you find a doc or memory describing About's photo as fading into the gradient ("mirrored from HowITeach", "three uses of this exact technique: Hero, HowITeach, About"), it's stale** — that fade is gone; only Hero and HowITeach still use it. Mobile (`≤800px`) just shrinks the same polaroid (`width: min(260px, 70%)` vs desktop's `min(320px, 80%)`) — no more full-width stretch/mask-removal special case, since there's no mask anywhere now. The section's own dark gradient background and the grid/column structure (no `.about-inner` wrapper, copy-left/photo-right) are untouched by this — only the photo's own treatment changed.
- **Bio text recolored for the dark background.** `renderMarkdown(content.bio)` output is unchanged — same field, same markdown pipeline — but `.bio :global(p)` changed from `--color-ink-soft` to `rgb(255 255 255 / 0.85)`, matching `HowITeach`'s `.how-i-teach-text` treatment for white-on-teal body copy.
- **New `.about-stamp` decoration**, reusing `public/images/heart_stamp.svg` (already used by `Services.astro`) via the same mask-image + `background-color` recolor technique as the site's other stamps. Tinted `var(--color-white)` here — vs `--color-tertiary` on Services' cream background — since white is what reads against About's dark teal. It's absolutely positioned inside a new `.about-title-row` wrapper that scopes it to just the `<h2>`, not the whole `.about-copy` column (which would also include the bio) — the same scoping choice `Services.astro`'s `.services-copy` makes for its own heart stamp.

`content/siteContent.json` gained top-level `howITeach`/`lessonsDifferent` keys, inserted right after `method`, matching the two new `SiteContent` fields.

**Admin editor**: the "Method Section" fieldset lost `lessonsSubtitle`/`teachingText`/`stepsLabel`/`methodSteps` — back to just visible/title/image/introSubtitle/introText/`methodItems` *at the time of this change*. (The image field and `introText` were later removed too, by the separate `redo-method-polaroid-grid` pass described in the Method paragraph above — the fieldset today is just visible/title/introSubtitle/`methodItems`.) Two new fieldsets replace them: "How I Teach Section" (visible, heading, text, its own image field) and "What Makes My Lessons Different Section" (visible, heading, a steps list). The steps-list admin JS was generalized from Method-specific names to reusable ones: `renderMethodSteps`/`addMethodStepRow`/`readMethodSteps` (hardcoded to `#methodSteps-list`) became `renderStepsList(containerId, steps)`/`addStepRow(list, step)`/`readStepsList(containerId)`, now also driving `#lessonsSteps-list` → `lessonsDifferent.steps`. Row class renamed `.method-step-row` → `.step-row` (cosmetic — no CSS rule existed for either name, so no visual change).

**`Services.astro`** was rebuilt to match `Ideas/Mockups/Services.jpg`, reusing the layout pattern `Method.astro` had *at the time* (this predates Method's later `redo-method-polaroid-grid` rewrite above — Method itself no longer has a `.method-top` or this overlap technique, but Services' own layout is unaffected by that later change): `.services-top` (title left in `.services-copy`, a circular `PlaceholderImage` portrait right) followed by `.services-grid` (the 4 service cards) that deliberately overlaps `.services-top` via a z-index + negative-margin technique (photo `z-index: 1`, card grid `z-index: 2` + `margin-top: -6rem`, so a card corner visibly cuts into the circle) — implemented correctly here on the first pass, unlike Method which needed a follow-up fix for the same bug back when it still used this technique. `ServicesContent` gained its own `image: string` (`services.image` in `content/siteContent.json`, seeded `/images/lara_about.jpg` — an independently owned field, not a reuse of another section's image; `method.image` itself was later removed when Method dropped its portrait entirely, see above), with a matching `image-field` block in the admin's "Services Section" fieldset. Title (`content.title`, "Ways to Learn With Me") goes through `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Learn"]` list — same precedent as Method's `["Method"]`/LessonsDifferent's `["different"]` — and the same `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)` treatment as the other three redone sections. `.services-grid` is `grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr)` (one row on desktop, per explicit request) rather than the previous wrapping `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...))`, collapsing to 2 then 1 column at narrower breakpoints.

Each `.service-card`'s icon circle cycles through 4 pastel tints via a positional `cardTints` array (`--color-primary-light`, `--color-secondary-light`, `--color-tertiary-light`, `--color-primary-light`) — same fixed-array-in-frontmatter convention as Pain Points'/LessonsDifferent's icon arrays, not admin-configurable. `--color-tertiary-light` (`src/styles/global.css`, `color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-tertiary) 35%, white)`) is a new derived token, needed as the 4th color in this cycle — same 35% convention as the existing `--color-primary-light`. A parallel `cardAccents` array (the plain saturated colors, not the light tints) drives two smaller decorations per card: a `.service-icon-badge` dot overlapping the icon circle's corner, and a `.service-divider` bar under the card title. The icon glyph itself stays a fixed `var(--color-primary-dark)` stroke regardless of card — **not** tinted per-card, matching the mockup's uniformly dark icon strokes. Card text is centered (`align-items: center; text-align: center`), a change from the previous left-aligned cards. `ServiceIcon.astro`'s `company` icon was redrawn from two building/office rectangles into a literal briefcase (rounded body, top handle arc, seam line, latch), since the old shape didn't read as "work" — the other 3 icons (`private`/`group`/`club`) are untouched.

Two new decorative stamps, using the same mask-image + `background-color` recolor technique as `italy_stamp.svg`/`spritz_stamp.svg` (fill baked into their own `<path style>`, not `currentColor`), both tinted `var(--color-tertiary)`: `public/images/heart_stamp.svg` (~0.877:1) bottom-right of the title/copy area, and `public/images/star_stamp.svg` (~0.767:1) top-left of the first card, sitting beside/outside the card rather than overlapping its opaque body.

**`Results.astro`** was rebuilt to match `Ideas/Mockups/Results.jpg`. `ResultsContent` (`{ title, image, beginner, intermediate, visible }`) is unchanged — this was purely a layout/styling pass, not a content-model change. `content/siteContent.json`'s `results.title` moved from the placeholder `"Results"` to the real copy `"What You Can Achieve"`, and `results.image` now points at a dedicated `/images/results.png` — same "mockup copy replaces foreign placeholder" pattern as every other redone section.

- **Two stacked rows, not one side-by-side grid.** The previous version was a single grid (image beside a title+cards column). Now `.results-top` (title left, image right — the same 2-column top-row idiom Method.astro used to have via its now-removed `.method-top`, see the Method paragraph above) sits above `.results-cards` (the two outcome cards, now full-width and stacked, no longer sharing the row with the image). Unlike Services (and Method's old layout), **there's no z-index/negative-margin overlap between the two rows here** — `.results-top` and `.results-cards` are a plain stacked layout; don't assume every redone section uses the overlap trick.
- Title goes through `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Achieve"]` list — same precedent as Method's `["Method"]`/Services' `["Learn"]` — in the same `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)` treatment as the other redone sections.
- **Cards restyled from a bordered tag-and-text card into a two-tone flex row.** Each `.results-card` is `overflow: hidden` + `border-radius: var(--radius-lg)` **on the outer card itself** (not on its two children) so the colored icon block and the pale text block read as one seamless rounded shape rather than two independently-rounded pieces. Inside: `.results-card-icon` (a fixed ~6.5rem-wide colored block, full card height, one of a potted-plant/tree SVG pair cycling by position — `cardIcons`/`cardColors` arrays in the frontmatter, same fixed-array-not-admin-configurable convention as Pain Points'/Services' icon arrays; colors cycle `--color-primary-light`/`--color-tertiary-light`) and `.results-card-body` (flex:1, `background: var(--color-secondary-pale)`, reusing Method's existing token rather than adding a new one). Unlike the inline hand-drawn icon paths used elsewhere (Pain Points, Services), `cardIcons` here holds file paths — `public/images/results_plant.svg`/`results_tree.svg`, two dedicated assets rendered via a plain `<img>` (through `withBase()`), not `set:html` on an inline `<svg>`. Both files are already white-filled (`fill:#ffffff` baked in), so no mask-recolor technique is needed — they just render as-is on the colored block. The bold-lead-text parser (`splitLeadBold()`, splits a leading `**bold**` markdown segment into a label + body) is unchanged — only its rendering changed, from a small uppercase-letterspaced tag to a plain bold line (`.results-label`, `font-weight:700; font-size:1.1rem`, no `text-transform`), matching the mockup's "bold sentence, then normal text" look.
- The decorative `.results-blob` background SVG (low-opacity teal blob) is untouched by this pass.

`Testimonials.astro` was redesigned to match `Ideas/Mockups/Reviews.jpg`, replacing the earlier speech-bubble-with-external-avatar treatment. `TestimonialsContent`/`Testimonial`/`TestimonialAvatar.astro` are all unchanged — this was a pure component rewrite, no content-model change. The heading now goes through `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Students"]` list (matches "What My Students Say" — same precedent as Method/Results/Services' titles), `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)`. Section background changed from `--color-cream` to `--color-secondary-pale` (reusing Method/Results' existing token, no new one added).

- **The avatar now lives inside the card, not beside it.** Each `.testimonial-card` is a white box containing (top to bottom): a small hand-drawn quote-mark SVG (two mirrored comma-blob paths, `var(--color-primary)` — not an emoji/font glyph), `.testimonial-text`, a `.testimonial-toggle` button, a divider, then a `.testimonial-meta` footer row. The old "avatar overlaps the card's top-left corner via negative margin" layout is gone. **The avatar is now cropped by its circle, not just centered inside it**: `.testimonial-avatar-circle` is `position: relative; overflow: hidden` (it used to center the avatar with flex, no cropping), and `:global(.testimonial-avatar)` is `position: absolute; left: 50%; bottom: 0; translate: -50% 0; width: 2.4rem` — horizontally centered, anchored to the circle's bottom edge, sized larger than the circle so `overflow: hidden` actually crops it rather than just fitting it. `.testimonial-avatar-circle` is shared verbatim by the modal's avatar slot (see below), so this cropping applies there too.
- **Clamp is unchanged; the toggle behavior is not — "Show more" now opens a modal instead of expanding the card inline.** Text is still clamped to 5 lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 5`), and the `scrollHeight`-vs-`clientHeight` overflow check that hides the toggle when a review doesn't actually overflow is unchanged. But the old `.is-expanded` class + inline clamp-lifting toggle is gone entirely — the button's label is a static "Show more" (it no longer flips to "Show less", since it doesn't toggle card state anymore) and its click handler calls `openModal(card)`. `openModal` reads the full text/name/date/avatar straight off the already-rendered card's DOM — `.testimonial-text`'s `textContent` still holds the complete unclamped string, since clamping is a pure CSS visual truncation, so no separate data attribute or duplicate copy was needed — and populates one shared modal per section (`#testimonial-modal-backdrop`, not one instance per card): a fixed backdrop + centered `.testimonial-modal-card` with a close (×) button, the quote-mark SVG repeated, the full text, and a `.testimonial-avatar-circle.testimonial-modal-avatar` (reusing the avatar-circle styling above) + name/date, populated by cloning the source card's avatar circle's `innerHTML`/`style` attribute. Closes via the × button, clicking the backdrop itself (checked via `event.target === modalBackdrop`, so clicks inside the card don't close it), or Escape. **CSS gotcha worth remembering**: the backdrop's own unconditional `display: flex` would override the HTML `hidden` attribute's default `display: none` (a plain rule beats the `[hidden]` UA style), so the modal rendered open-but-empty on load until scoped to `.testimonial-modal-backdrop:not([hidden]) { display: flex }` — keep this pattern in mind for any other `hidden`-driven show/hide element in this codebase.
- **New divider**: an inline `<svg viewBox="0 0 300 16">` with a single path (`M0,2 H50 L65,15 L80,2 H300`) — a horizontal line with a downward notch built into the path itself, read as "a line with a speech-bubble tail" — replaces the old `::before` CSS-triangle tail. It sits directly above the footer row rather than below the whole card. `.testimonial-divider` also carries `margin-top: auto`: since `.testimonials-row` stretches all cards to equal height and `.testimonial-card` is a column flex container, that margin eats the free space above the divider, pinning divider+footer to the bottom of every card regardless of review length (otherwise a short review like "prova" left blank space below its footer instead of lining up with longer cards').
- **New footer row** (`.testimonial-meta`, now inside the card): a colored `.testimonial-avatar-circle` (containing `TestimonialAvatar`) next to stacked `.testimonial-name` (bold) / `.testimonial-date` (smaller, muted) — replaces the old single-line `justify-content: space-between` meta row. Avatar circle color comes from `avatarTints`, a fixed 3-entry `{bg, fg}` array (primary/secondary/tertiary, light-tint background + solid foreground) cycled by the item's position in `content.items` — **not** admin-editable and **not** tied to `gender` (`gender` still only picks the silhouette shape via `TestimonialAvatar`), same fixed-positional-array convention as Pain Points'/Services' icon arrays. The secondary (yellow) entry's `bg` is `--color-secondary-pale` (not `--color-secondary-light`) — lightened per feedback that the yellow tint read too saturated against the white card; its `fg` is unchanged at `--color-secondary`, and the other two tints (primary, tertiary) are unchanged.

  - Two arrow `<button>`s (`.testimonials-arrow--prev`/`--next`, solid `--color-tertiary` circles with an inline white chevron), absolutely positioned at the row's vertical center.
  - Dots (`.testimonials-dots`, one per `content.items` entry) below the row.

#### Carousel mechanics: center-focus, exactly-3-visible design (branch `redo-testimonials-carousel`)

The carousel was reworked a second time, replacing a simple `overflow-x:auto` strip (uniform-sized cards, `scrollBy`-based arrows, `IntersectionObserver`-driven active dot) with a "center-focus" design where the centered card visibly grows and its neighbors shrink as the user scrolls or drags — not just a discrete snap-and-highlight. The avatar-in-card layout, modal "Show more" system, and footer/divider markup described above are untouched by this pass.

- **`--tcard-w`/`--tcard-gap` (new custom properties on `.testimonials`) are the single source of truth** for card width, card gap, and viewport width, so the three can't drift apart: `.testimonials-viewport` (new wrapper, `overflow: hidden`) is sized to exactly `calc(3 * var(--tcard-w) + 2 * var(--tcard-gap))` — precisely 3 cards, with no partial 4th card peeking at the edge, at rest.
- **`.testimonials-row` gets `padding-inline: calc(var(--tcard-w) + var(--tcard-gap))`** — one card+gap of blank space on each end — which is what lets the very first and very last real card still scroll all the way to dead-center, even though nothing precedes/follows them.
- **`.testimonial-card` has a fixed `height: 21rem`** (19rem at ≤700px), previously auto-sized to content — cards are now uniform size regardless of review length. This is layout-level sizing, separate from the dynamic visual scaling below.
- **Dynamic center-focus scaling**: a `scroll` listener on `.testimonials-row`, rAF-throttled (`ticking` flag), computes each card's horizontal distance from the row's viewport center on every tick and sets an inline `transform: scale(...)` (interpolated from ~1.15 at dead-center down to ~0.82 at the viewport edge, via `applyScale()`) plus a matching opacity falloff (1 down to 0.6) — so focus continuously tracks scroll position rather than snapping between two states.
- **`centerCard(card, behavior)` / `closestIndex()`** are the shared primitives: `centerCard` computes the `getBoundingClientRect()` delta between a card's center and the row's center and scrolls by it; `closestIndex` finds whichever card is nearest the row's center right now. Arrow clicks, dot clicks, window resize (re-centers the previously-active card), and the initial on-load centering (card 0) all go through these two functions instead of duplicating the centering math.
- **No infinite loop, intentionally.** Prev/next arrows clamp at the array bounds (`Math.max(idx - 1, 0)` / `Math.min(idx + 1, cards.length - 1)`). An earlier iteration of this carousel used clone-based infinite scrolling (cards duplicated at each end, with a silent scroll-jump to fake an endless loop) but it was dropped per explicit follow-up feedback — it was buggy and added complexity nobody asked to keep. Don't reintroduce it without discussing first.
- **CSS gotcha**: `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` was tried and actively fought the JS-computed `centerCard()` offset, silently re-snapping to the wrong (adjacent) card after every programmatic scroll. Settled on `scroll-snap-type: x proximity` — soft enough to not override programmatic centering while still giving free-drag scrolling a gentle snap.
- The active dot is now set inside the same scroll-tick handler (`setActive(closestIndex())`), not a separate `IntersectionObserver` — if you find a doc or memory describing an `IntersectionObserver`-based active-dot tracker for this carousel, it's stale.

### Navigation: sticky horizontal top bar

`src/components/Navbar.astro` is a fixed, full-width top bar (`position: fixed; top:0`, height `var(--navbar-height)`) — it replaced an earlier fixed vertical icon dock pinned to the left edge; if you find any doc or memory of that dock, it's stale, don't resurrect it without discussing first. Layout is a 3-column CSS grid (`1fr auto 1fr`): a left link group, a centered logo/wordmark, a right link group. `NavIcon.astro` is no longer used by the nav (plain text links now, matching the reference mockup) — the component itself is kept, unused, same precedent as `SectionKicker`.

**Colors: the bar itself is `--color-primary`, not white.** Link text and the logo default to `--color-white`; the active link (and hover) switch to `--color-secondary`. This is the one place on the site where a link's "active" state is communicated by a color swap rather than a background/shape change — keep that in mind if this pattern is extended elsewhere.

- **Links**: only 6 of the page's sections are linked — Home, Method, Services, Reviews, About me, Contacts (`pain-points` is intentionally *not* linked, though it still renders on the page; `testimonials` *was* excluded under this same precedent but was swapped in for the old `results` link per explicit request — Results itself is still intentionally not linked). `Navbar.astro`'s own `links` array is the single source of truth; it's split in half at render time into `leftLinks`/`rightLinks` for the two grid columns.
- **Logo**: derived from `meta.siteName` the same way `Hero.astro` derives its `<h1>` (split on `" with "`), rendered via `SpritzTitle` at a small size — styled with `--font-script` (a fineline cursive, see the typography note below), distinct from Hero's bold display title treatment.  This is why `Navbar.astro` now declares a real `Props { meta: SiteMeta }` — it previously declared no props at all and silently ignored the `meta` prop `Layout.astro` was already passing it.
- **Centering trick**: `#navbar-links` (the `<nav>` wrapping both link `<ul>`s) uses `display: contents` on desktop so its two `<ul>` children become direct items of the parent grid (placed in column 1 and column 3) while the logo occupies column 2 — this is what lets the logo sit visually *between* two non-adjacent link groups that share one semantic `<nav>` landmark. The mobile media query cancels `display: contents` back to `display: block` so the nav becomes a normal stacked dropdown panel instead.
- **All 3 items must be pinned to `grid-row: 1` explicitly.** Leaving row auto (as an early version of this component did) causes a real CSS Grid footgun: the logo's *explicit* `grid-column: 2` gets placed first by the browser's sparse auto-placement algorithm, which then advances its placement cursor *past* column 1 for that row — so the left `<ul>` (needing column 1) can no longer be auto-placed in row 1 and silently drops into a second implicit row instead, well below the logo. The symptom was exactly the bug reported against this component: nav links rendering visibly lower than the logo despite `align-items: center` looking correct on paper. `grid-row: 1` on all three (`.navbar-list--left`, `.navbar-logo`, `.navbar-list--right`) forces single-row placement and fixes it outright — no `translate` hacks needed once this is set correctly.
- **Mobile**: below 760px, the two link groups collapse behind a hamburger button (`#navbar-toggle`) that toggles a `position: fixed` dropdown panel spanning the viewport width under the bar; an inline `<script>` toggles `.is-open`, closes on link click / outside click / Escape, and flips the 3 toggle bars into an X via CSS.
- The active section is tracked with the same `IntersectionObserver` pattern as before (rootMargin `-40% 0px -40% 0px`), but `sectionIds` is no longer a second hardcoded array kept in sync by hand — it's derived directly from whichever elements have a `data-section` attribute in the DOM (i.e. from the same 6-link array used to render the nav), removing the drift risk that existed previously.
- `--navbar-height` (`src/styles/global.css`) is now actually load-bearing: the bar's own height, and `html { scroll-padding-top: var(--navbar-height) }` (added alongside this redesign) so anchor-link scrolling lands below the fixed bar instead of under it.

### Icon components

Two components share one convention — an inline SVG wrapper (`viewBox 0 0 24 24`, `stroke=currentColor`, path fragments injected via `set:html`) keyed by a string prop through a `Record<string, string>` lookup, with a safe fallback if the key is unrecognized:

- `SocialIcon.astro` — keyed by social platform (`platform` prop): instagram, facebook, whatsapp, tiktok, email, website.
- `NavIcon.astro` — keyed by section id (`section` prop): hero, about, pain-points, method, results, testimonials, contact. Currently unused (see "Navigation" above), kept rather than deleted.

When adding a new social platform or page section, add its SVG fragment to the relevant component's `icons` map rather than creating a new icon component.

Testimonial avatars are a deliberately separate, simpler convention — don't fold them into the `set:html` pattern above:

- `TestimonialAvatar.astro` renders one of three inline SVGs (originally `public/images/testimonial_{male,female,other}.svg`) keyed by each testimonial's `gender` enum, using `fill="currentColor"` so the avatar tracks `--color-primary-dark` via a `color:` CSS property set on `.testimonial-avatar` in `Testimonials.astro` — this is what makes the avatars respond to admin color changes instead of staying a hardcoded hex baked into the SVG file.
- Its sizing CSS in `Testimonials.astro` uses `:global(.testimonial-avatar)` rather than a plain scoped selector, because Astro's scoped-style hashing doesn't cross into a child component's own template. Keep this in mind for any future child-component pattern — a plain (non-`:global`) selector targeting a child component's root element silently does nothing.
- These are fixed placeholder avatars, not user-uploaded photos — `gender` is a closed 3-value enum edited via a `<select>` in the admin editor (same UI pattern as the social-platform select), so there's no upload/`pendingUploads` handling for this field.

### Italy motifs: `italy.svg` (outline) and `italy_stamp.svg` (stamp) — same mask-recolor trick, two assets

`public/images/italy.svg` (a solid white-filled Italy silhouette, not a thin outline) has its fill color baked into the SVG's own `<path style>` (not `currentColor`), so it can't be recolored with a simple CSS `color`/`fill` override. ### The 3-color brand system — read this before touching any color

The entire palette is derived from exactly 3 admin-editable tokens. **Never hardcode a 4th brand color or a raw hex value in a component** — if you need a new tone, add it as a `color-mix()` derived token in `:root` (`src/styles/global.css`) so it inherits admin edits automatically.

- Base tokens: `--color-primary`, `--color-secondary`, `--color-tertiary` (plus the fixed `--color-white`). Everything else is derived once in `:root`, e.g. `--color-primary-dark` (80% primary/black), `--color-primary-light` (35% primary/white), `--color-secondary-light` (65% secondary/white — used for accents that used to be "yellow": Hero's title accent line/Italy flourish, Method's subtitle/steps-label), `--color-cream` (6% **secondary**/white — deliberately derived from secondary, not primary), `--color-ink` (40% primary/black — body text is deliberately primary-tinted, not neutral grey), `--color-ink-soft` (70% primary/black). `--shadow-soft`/`--shadow-lift` are also `color-mix()` expressions so shadows track the live palette instead of being hardcoded `rgb()`.
- The 3 base values live in content, not CSS: `SiteMeta` (`src/types/content.ts`) has `primaryColor`/`secondaryColor`/`tertiaryColor`, seeded in `content/siteContent.json`. `src/layouts/Layout.astro` injects them as real custom properties via an inline `style` attribute on `<html>` (`--color-primary:${meta.primaryColor}; ...`) — inline style always wins the cascade, so this overrides the `:root` defaults, and every derived token recomputes automatically since they all reference these same var names. `<meta name="theme-color">` reads `primaryColor` directly. There used to be a separate `meta.themeColor` field for this exact purpose — it drifted into an orphaned, type-less JSON key (never read by any component) and was removed; `primaryColor` has done this job directly all along.
- The admin editor's Site Info fieldset exposes 3 `<input type="color">` pickers (`meta.primaryColor`/`secondaryColor`/`tertiaryColor`) — required no new JS, since scalar fields already go through the generic populate/read path. They have their own CSS rule (`.panel input[type="color"]`) with a fixed `width: 4.5rem; height: 3rem` box, separate from the generic text-input rule — sharing that rule (`width:100%; padding:0.6rem 0.8rem`) used to squash the native swatch down to nearly invisible.
- Every component was mechanically renamed from the old ~10-color palette (`--color-teal`, `--color-yellow`, `--color-ocher`, `--color-red`, etc.) to the new var names. `CustomCursor.astro`'s ice/orange-slice fallback illustrations got the same var renames but are decorative and exempt from strict brand-contrast rules. Actual multi-color illustration image files (cursor sprites, hero brand mark) were left untouched — they keep their natural hues regardless of the brand tokens.

### Per-section visibility toggles, and Results' title field

`PainPointsContent`, `MethodContent`, `HowITeachContent`, `LessonsDifferentContent`, `ServicesContent`, `ResultsContent`, `TestimonialsContent`, and `AboutContent` (`src/types/content.ts`) each carry a `visible: boolean` field, seeded `true` in `content/siteContent.json`. `index.astro` wraps each of those 8 section invocations in `{content.X.visible && <X content={content.X} />}` — Testimonials keeps its separate pre-existing `items.length > 0` guard on top of this, the two checks compose fine. Hero and Contact deliberately have no toggle — they're the page's structural start/end and are never meant to be hidden. Each admin fieldset got a matching `<label class="checkbox-label"><input type="checkbox" data-field="X.visible" /> ...</label>`.

Adding the checkbox required one small generic addition to the admin's `[data-field]` scalar-field JS (`populateScalarFields`/`readScalarFields`, driven by `getPath`/`setPath`): a branch checking `el.type === "checkbox"` to read/write `.checked` instead of `.value`. This is the only new JS logic the whole feature needed — every other new field, including the checkboxes' labels and Results' new title input, reuses the existing generic path unmodified.

Results was the only section with no editable title at all — it had a hardcoded `<h2>Results</h2>`. (Hero's heading comes from "Site name" and About's from "Name", both already editable, so those were intentionally left alone.) `ResultsContent` gained a `title: string`, rendered as `<h2>{content.title}</h2>` in `Results.astro`, with a new "Heading" text input (`data-field="results.title"`) in the admin's Results fieldset.
