## Landing Page Architecture

`src/pages/index.astro` composes the one-page site from section components, in this order: Hero → PainPoints → Method → HowITeach → LessonsDifferent → Services → Results → Testimonials → About → Contact. About sits just before Contact by design (it reads better as a "who's behind this" beat right before the CTA, rather than as a second impression right after Hero). Testimonials renders nothing (no `<section>` at all) when `content.testimonials.items` is empty, so the section can be emptied out via the admin editor without leaving a gap in the page or the nav.

Sections no longer show a small "eyebrow" pill above their `<h2>` — `Services.astro`, `Testimonials.astro`, `About.astro`, `Contact.astro`, and now `Hero.astro` each render only their real heading now (`HeroContent.eyebrow` was removed from the type/JSON/admin editor along with it — it was already empty and had no slot in the redesigned Hero). `SectionKicker.astro` still exists as a component but nothing imports it; it was kept in case the pattern is wanted again rather than deleted outright.

`Method.astro`, `HowITeach.astro`, and `LessonsDifferent.astro` are **three separate top-level sections** (`Method.astro` matches `Ideas/Mockups/Method.jpg`; the other two match `Ideas/Mockups/Method2.jpg`) — not, as an earlier iteration on this branch had it, "teach"/"timeline" bands stacked inside one `<section id="method">`. That first attempt nested the two bands inside Method's own padded section, which visually read as "inside the My Method background" rather than truly full-width like Hero/About; being full-bleed *CSS* (a 2-column grid not wrapped in `.container`) wasn't enough while they were still descendants of a padded parent section. The fix was structural, not CSS: each band became its own top-level `<section>` (`how-i-teach`, `lessons-different`), rendered as a sibling of `Method` in `index.astro`, with its own content-model interface, its own `visible` toggle, and its own admin fieldset — matching how every other section already works. **If you find a doc or memory describing "part 1"/"part 2" bands inside one Method section, it's stale — don't resurrect that structure without discussing it first.** Neither new section was added to `Navbar.astro`'s 6-link nav — same precedent as `pain-points`/`testimonials`: they render and are individually toggleable, but don't get a nav link or scroll-spy entry.

**`Method.astro`** is back to just its original title+photo+card-grid content: a single `<section id="method">` on a pale background (`background: var(--color-secondary-pale)`), `.method-top` (a 2-column grid: `.method-copy` — title/subtitle/intro text — left, a circular `PlaceholderImage` portrait right) plus a 6-card `.method-grid` below it that deliberately overlaps `.method-top`: `.method-portrait` (`z-index: 1`) is taller than `.method-copy`, so `.method-grid` (`z-index: 2`, `margin-top: -6rem`) tucks its top row up under that overflow, rendering in front of the photo so a card corner visibly cuts into the circle — layering via z-index + negative margin, gated to desktop, collapsing to a non-overlapping single column at ≤800px. The title uses `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Method"]` highlight list, `--font-display` (Bebas Neue), uppercase. `MethodContent` (`src/types/content.ts`) is `{ title, image, introSubtitle, introText, items, visible }` — the teaching/timeline fields that used to live here (`teachingText`, `stepsLabel`, `steps`, `lessonsSubtitle`) are gone from the type entirely, not just unrendered; they moved into the two new interfaces below, and `MethodStep` no longer exists as a type.

**`HowITeach.astro`** (`<section id="how-i-teach">`) — full-bleed 2-column grid (NOT wrapped in `.container`, same pattern as `Hero.astro`'s `.hero-inner`/`About.astro`'s `.about-inner`), now a genuine top-level sibling section with no ancestor section padding — this is what actually fixes the full-width issue, not the grid CSS alone. Left column: `content.image` via `PlaceholderImage variant="rounded"` — `HowITeachContent.image` is its **own** field (seeded `/images/lara_about.jpg`), **not** a reuse of `method.image` the way the earlier iteration did; every section now owns its content independently. Its right edge fades into the gradient panel next to it via `mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, black 55%, transparent 100%)` on `:global(.placeholder-image)` — explicitly mirroring Hero's own portrait-fade technique (`Hero` uses `to left` since its photo is on the right; this one uses `to right` since its photo is on the left). **The gradient background lives on the outer `.how-i-teach` section itself, not on `.how-i-teach-copy`** — Hero's exact gradient formula verbatim (`--color-primary` base + `linear-gradient(160deg, ...)` of `color-mix()`d primary/primary-dark) is set on the section, so masking the photo to transparent on the left reveals the *same* teal gradient underneath rather than blank space. (An earlier pass put the gradient on `.how-i-teach-copy` instead, which meant the photo's fade revealed whatever sat behind it — nothing — so it faded to white instead of blending into teal; moving the background up to the section fixed it and made this a genuine structural match to `Hero.astro`, whose gradient has likewise always lived on the outer `.hero` section, not a sub-column.) `.how-i-teach-copy` itself carries no background of its own — just white text, padded via a nested `.container`. Heading (`content.heading`) uses the same treatment as `Method.astro`'s title — `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)`, bumped up from an earlier smaller `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)` — with `"teach"` hardcoded as the `splitHighlighted()` highlight word (`var(--color-secondary)`). Body = `content.text`. `public/images/spritz_stamp.svg` sits bottom-right as a decorative white mask (same `mask-image` + `background-color: var(--color-white)` technique as `Hero.astro`'s `.hero-italy`), now tilted `rotate: 12deg` (previously untilted). `HowITeachContent`: `{ heading, text, image, visible }`.

**`LessonsDifferent.astro`** (`<section id="lessons-different">`) — white background, centered heading using the same Method-title treatment (`--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)`, also bumped up from a smaller size), `"different"` hardcoded as the `splitHighlighted()` highlight word. Below it, `content.steps.map(...)` (`LessonsStep = { title, description }` — same shape as the removed `MethodStep`) renders a 4-column timeline redesigned to match the mockup structurally. Per step, top to bottom: a small colored `.timeline-circle` holding the step **number** (`01`–`04`, white text) — previously the circle held an icon and the number sat above it as plain text; a short vertical dashed `.timeline-connector` (new element, `border-left: 2px dashed`); the step's icon, now an **outline** SVG (`stroke={timelineColors[i]}`) below the connector — previously a solid white-filled icon sat inside the circle; then title/description. Step content (`.timeline-step`) is **centered** (`align-items: center; text-align: center`) — a left-aligned pass (`align-items: flex-start`, with a compensating `margin-left` on `.timeline-connector` to keep it visually under the circle) was tried and then reverted back to centered per Alessandro's final read of the mockup: numbers/icons should sit centered above their paragraph. The connector has no horizontal offset of its own kind; if you find one, it's a stale leftover from the left-aligned attempt. Circle and icon still share one color per step via the same positional `timelineColors` array as before (`--color-primary`, `--color-primary-dark`, `--color-secondary`, `--color-tertiary`). The horizontal dashed connector between circles (`.timeline::before`) is unchanged in technique (absolutely positioned `border-top: 2px dashed`, z-index layering). The `italy_stamp.svg` decoration moved from bottom-right to **top-left** (`left: 0; top: -1rem`), still tinted `var(--color-primary)`. `LessonsDifferentContent`: `{ heading, steps: LessonsStep[], visible }`.

`content/siteContent.json` gained top-level `howITeach`/`lessonsDifferent` keys, inserted right after `method`, matching the two new `SiteContent` fields.

**Admin editor**: the "Method Section" fieldset lost `lessonsSubtitle`/`teachingText`/`stepsLabel`/`methodSteps` — back to just visible/title/image/introSubtitle/introText/`methodItems`. Two new fieldsets replace them: "How I Teach Section" (visible, heading, text, its own image field) and "What Makes My Lessons Different Section" (visible, heading, a steps list). The steps-list admin JS was generalized from Method-specific names to reusable ones: `renderMethodSteps`/`addMethodStepRow`/`readMethodSteps` (hardcoded to `#methodSteps-list`) became `renderStepsList(containerId, steps)`/`addStepRow(list, step)`/`readStepsList(containerId)`, now also driving `#lessonsSteps-list` → `lessonsDifferent.steps`. Row class renamed `.method-step-row` → `.step-row` (cosmetic — no CSS rule existed for either name, so no visual change).

**`Services.astro`** was rebuilt to match `Ideas/Mockups/Services.jpg`, reusing Method's proven layout pattern rather than inventing a new one: `.services-top` (title left in `.services-copy`, a circular `PlaceholderImage` portrait right — same `"circle"` variant as Method's) followed by `.services-grid` (the 4 service cards) that deliberately overlaps `.services-top` via the identical z-index + negative-margin technique already fixed in Method after earlier feedback (photo `z-index: 1`, card grid `z-index: 2` + `margin-top: -6rem`, so a card corner visibly cuts into the circle) — implemented correctly here on the first pass, unlike Method which needed a follow-up fix for the same bug. `ServicesContent` gained its own `image: string` (`services.image` in `content/siteContent.json`, seeded `/images/lara_about.jpg` — same placeholder-until-dedicated-photo pattern as `method.image`/`howITeach.image`, but an independently owned field, not a reuse), with a matching `image-field` block in the admin's "Services Section" fieldset. Title (`content.title`, "Ways to Learn With Me") goes through `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Learn"]` list — same precedent as Method's `["Method"]`/LessonsDifferent's `["different"]` — and the same `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)` treatment as the other three redone sections. `.services-grid` is `grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr)` (one row on desktop, per explicit request) rather than the previous wrapping `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...))`, collapsing to 2 then 1 column at narrower breakpoints.

Each `.service-card`'s icon circle cycles through 4 pastel tints via a positional `cardTints` array (`--color-primary-light`, `--color-secondary-light`, `--color-tertiary-light`, `--color-primary-light`) — same fixed-array-in-frontmatter convention as Pain Points'/LessonsDifferent's icon arrays, not admin-configurable. `--color-tertiary-light` (`src/styles/global.css`, `color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-tertiary) 35%, white)`) is a new derived token, needed as the 4th color in this cycle — same 35% convention as the existing `--color-primary-light`. A parallel `cardAccents` array (the plain saturated colors, not the light tints) drives two smaller decorations per card: a `.service-icon-badge` dot overlapping the icon circle's corner, and a `.service-divider` bar under the card title. The icon glyph itself stays a fixed `var(--color-primary-dark)` stroke regardless of card — **not** tinted per-card, matching the mockup's uniformly dark icon strokes. Card text is centered (`align-items: center; text-align: center`), a change from the previous left-aligned cards. `ServiceIcon.astro`'s `company` icon was redrawn from two building/office rectangles into a literal briefcase (rounded body, top handle arc, seam line, latch), since the old shape didn't read as "work" — the other 3 icons (`private`/`group`/`club`) are untouched.

Two new decorative stamps, using the same mask-image + `background-color` recolor technique as `italy_stamp.svg`/`spritz_stamp.svg` (fill baked into their own `<path style>`, not `currentColor`), both tinted `var(--color-tertiary)`: `public/images/heart_stamp.svg` (~0.877:1) bottom-right of the title/copy area, and `public/images/star_stamp.svg` (~0.767:1) top-left of the first card, sitting beside/outside the card rather than overlapping its opaque body.

**`Results.astro`** was rebuilt to match `Ideas/Mockups/Results.jpg`. `ResultsContent` (`{ title, image, beginner, intermediate, visible }`) is unchanged — this was purely a layout/styling pass, not a content-model change. `content/siteContent.json`'s `results.title` moved from the placeholder `"Results"` to the real copy `"What You Can Achieve"`, and `results.image` now points at a dedicated `/images/results.png` — same "mockup copy replaces foreign placeholder" pattern as every other redone section.

- **Two stacked rows, not one side-by-side grid.** The previous version was a single grid (image beside a title+cards column). Now `.results-top` (title left, image right — same 2-column idiom as `Method.astro`'s `.method-top`) sits above `.results-cards` (the two outcome cards, now full-width and stacked, no longer sharing the row with the image). Unlike Method/Services, **there's no z-index/negative-margin overlap between the two rows here** — `.results-top` and `.results-cards` are a plain stacked layout; don't assume every redone section uses the overlap trick.
- Title goes through `splitHighlighted()` with a hardcoded `["Achieve"]` list — same precedent as Method's `["Method"]`/Services' `["Learn"]` — in the same `--font-display`, uppercase, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)` treatment as the other redone sections.
- **Cards restyled from a bordered tag-and-text card into a two-tone flex row.** Each `.results-card` is `overflow: hidden` + `border-radius: var(--radius-lg)` **on the outer card itself** (not on its two children) so the colored icon block and the pale text block read as one seamless rounded shape rather than two independently-rounded pieces. Inside: `.results-card-icon` (a fixed ~6.5rem-wide colored block, full card height, one of a potted-plant/tree SVG pair cycling by position — `cardIcons`/`cardColors` arrays in the frontmatter, same fixed-array-not-admin-configurable convention as Pain Points'/Services' icon arrays; colors cycle `--color-primary-light`/`--color-tertiary-light`). Unlike the inline hand-drawn icon paths used elsewhere (Pain Points, Services, LessonsDifferent), `cardIcons` here holds file paths — `public/images/results_plant.svg`/`results_tree.svg`, two dedicated assets rendered via a plain `<img>` (through `withBase()`), not `set:html` on an inline `<svg>`. Both files are already white-filled (`fill:#ffffff` baked in), so no mask-recolor technique is needed — they just render as-is on the colored block. and `.results-card-body` (flex:1, `background: var(--color-secondary-pale)`, reusing Method's existing token rather than adding a new one). The bold-lead-text parser (`splitLeadBold()`, splits a leading `**bold**` markdown segment into a label + body) is unchanged — only its rendering changed, from a small uppercase-letterspaced tag to a plain bold line (`.results-label`, `font-weight:700; font-size:1.1rem`, no `text-transform`), matching the mockup's "bold sentence, then normal text" look.
- The decorative `.results-blob` background SVG (low-opacity teal blob) is untouched by this pass.

`Testimonials.astro`'s card is a speech-bubble treatment matching the original `Ideas/testimonials.jpg` mockup: each `.testimonial-card` is a flex row where a small avatar (`.testimonial-avatar`, ~4.5rem) overlaps the left edge of a rounded `.testimonial-bubble` via negative margin, with a CSS-triangle `::before` as the bubble's "tail". (An earlier attempt on this branch tried a large background-silhouette treatment instead — abandoned in favor of the bubble, don't resurrect it.)

- Review text is clamped to 3 lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 3`) with a "Read more" button that toggles `.is-expanded` to lift the clamp and grow the card ("Show less" to re-collapse). An inline `<script>` compares each card's `scrollHeight`/`clientHeight` on load and hides the toggle entirely when a review doesn't actually overflow 3 lines, so short reviews never show a pointless button.
- The same script also redirects vertical wheel input on `.testimonials-row` into horizontal scrolling (`row.scrollLeft += event.deltaY`, with `preventDefault()`), since the row is a horizontally-scrolling strip and requiring shift+scroll or a scrollbar drag to browse it would be unintuitive.

### Navigation: sticky horizontal top bar

`src/components/Navbar.astro` is a fixed, full-width top bar (`position: fixed; top:0`, height `var(--navbar-height)`) — it replaced an earlier fixed vertical icon dock pinned to the left edge; if you find any doc or memory of that dock, it's stale, don't resurrect it without discussing first. Layout is a 3-column CSS grid (`1fr auto 1fr`): a left link group, a centered logo/wordmark, a right link group. `NavIcon.astro` is no longer used by the nav (plain text links now, matching the reference mockup) — the component itself is kept, unused, same precedent as `SectionKicker`.

**Colors: the bar itself is `--color-primary`, not white.** Link text and the logo default to `--color-white`; the active link (and hover) switch to `--color-secondary`. This is the one place on the site where a link's "active" state is communicated by a color swap rather than a background/shape change — keep that in mind if this pattern is extended elsewhere.

- **Links**: only 6 of the page's sections are linked — Home, Method, Services, Results, About me, Contacts (`pain-points` and `testimonials` are intentionally *not* linked, though those sections still render on the page). `Navbar.astro`'s own `links` array is the single source of truth; it's split in half at render time into `leftLinks`/`rightLinks` for the two grid columns.
- **Logo**: derived from `meta.siteName` the same way `Hero.astro` derives its `<h1>` (split on `" with "`), rendered via `SpritzTitle` at a small size — styled with `--font-script` (a fineline cursive, see the typography note below), distinct from Hero's bold display title treatment.  This is why `Navbar.astro` now declares a real `Props { meta: SiteMeta }` — it previously declared no props at all and silently ignored the `meta` prop `Layout.astro` was already passing it.
- **Centering trick**: `#navbar-links` (the `<nav>` wrapping both link `<ul>`s) uses `display: contents` on desktop so its two `<ul>` children become direct items of the parent grid (placed in column 1 and column 3) while the logo occupies column 2 — this is what lets the logo sit visually *between* two non-adjacent link groups that share one semantic `<nav>` landmark. The mobile media query cancels `display: contents` back to `display: block` so the nav becomes a normal stacked dropdown panel instead.
- **All 3 items must be pinned to `grid-row: 1` explicitly.** Leaving row auto (as an early version of this component did) causes a real CSS Grid footgun: the logo's *explicit* `grid-column: 2` gets placed first by the browser's sparse auto-placement algorithm, which then advances its placement cursor *past* column 1 for that row — so the left `<ul>` (needing column 1) can no longer be auto-placed in row 1 and silently drops into a second implicit row instead, well below the logo. The symptom was exactly the bug reported against this component: nav links rendering visibly lower than the logo despite `align-items: center` looking correct on paper. `grid-row: 1` on all three (`.navbar-list--left`, `.navbar-logo`, `.navbar-list--right`) forces single-row placement and fixes it outright — no `translate` hacks needed once this is set correctly.
- **Mobile**: below 760px, the two link groups collapse behind a hamburger button (`#navbar-toggle`) that toggles a `position: fixed` dropdown panel spanning the viewport width under the bar; an inline `<script>` toggles `.is-open`, closes on link click / outside click / Escape, and flips the 3 toggle bars into an X via CSS.
- The active section is tracked with the same `IntersectionObserver` pattern as before (rootMargin `-40% 0px -40% 0px`), but `sectionIds` is no longer a second hardcoded array kept in sync by hand — it's derived directly from whichever elements have a `data-section` attribute in the DOM (i.e. from the same 6-link array used to render the nav), removing the drift risk that existed previously.
- `--navbar-height` (`src/styles/global.css`) is now actually load-bearing: the bar's own height, and `html { scroll-padding-top: var(--navbar-height) }` (added alongside this redesign) so anchor-link scrolling lands below the fixed bar instead of under it.

### Icon components

Two components share one convention — an inline SVG wrapper (`viewBox 0 0 24 24`, `stroke=currentColor`, path fragments injected via `set:html`) keyed by a string prop through a `Record<string, string>` lookup, with a safe fallback if the key is unrecognized:

- `SocialIcon.astro` — keyed by social platform (`platform` prop): instagram, facebook, whatsapp, tiktok, email, website.
- `NavIcon.astro` — keyed by section id (`section` prop): hero, about, pain-points, method, results, testimonials, contact. Currently unused (see "Navigation" above), kept rather than deleted.

When adding a new social platform or page section, add its SVG fragment to the relevant component's `icons` map rather than creating a new icon component.

Testimonial avatars are a deliberately separate, simpler convention — don't fold them into the `set:html` pattern above:

- `TestimonialAvatar.astro` renders one of three inline SVGs (originally `public/images/testimonial_{male,female,other}.svg`) keyed by each testimonial's `gender` enum, using `fill="currentColor"` so the avatar tracks `--color-primary-dark` via a `color:` CSS property set on `.testimonial-avatar` in `Testimonials.astro` — this is what makes the avatars respond to admin color changes instead of staying a hardcoded hex baked into the SVG file.
- Its sizing CSS in `Testimonials.astro` uses `:global(.testimonial-avatar)` rather than a plain scoped selector, because Astro's scoped-style hashing doesn't cross into a child component's own template. Keep this in mind for any future child-component pattern — a plain (non-`:global`) selector targeting a child component's root element silently does nothing.
- These are fixed placeholder avatars, not user-uploaded photos — `gender` is a closed 3-value enum edited via a `<select>` in the admin editor (same UI pattern as the social-platform select), so there's no upload/`pendingUploads` handling for this field.

### Hero: uppercase title, body text field, square fading photo, top-right social buttons

`Hero.astro` was redesigned to match `Ideas/Mockups/Hero.jpg`. Notable changes from the previous version:

- `<h1>` is `text-transform: uppercase`, set in `--font-display` (see the typography note below), big (`clamp(3.6rem, 7.5vw, 6.2rem)`), and `align-items: center` (the flex-column cross-axis) so the two lines share a common center axis rather than a common left edge — a deliberate choice even though it means the title's alignment doesn't match the left-aligned body text/CTA below it. The second line ("with Lara", derived from `meta.siteName` the same way as before) opts back out to `--font-script` at `0.65em` — a fineline cursive contrasting the bold caps above it, not the same font as the caps line.
- `HeroContent` gained a `text: string` field (rendered as `.hero-text`, `white-space: pre-line` so literal `\n` line breaks in the JSON are preserved without needing markdown) — the body copy shown under the title, which the mockup has but the previous Hero didn't model at all. Admin editor: a new textarea (`data-field="hero.text"`) in the Hero fieldset.
- **Full-bleed layout, not a centered grid inside `.container`.** `Hero.astro` now follows the same full-bleed pattern `About.astro` established: `.hero-inner` is a 2-column grid that is *not* wrapped in `.container` (`grid-template-columns: minmax(320px, 50%) 1fr; align-items: stretch`), so the right column can reach the true viewport edge. `.hero-copy` (left column) carries its own `.container` class plus all the padding (including the navbar-height top offset) that used to live on `.hero` itself — `.hero` and `.hero-inner` now carry no padding of their own. `.hero-portrait` (right column) stretches to the row's full height via `align-items: stretch` and gets `width/height:100%; object-fit:cover; border-radius:0` via a `:global(.placeholder-image)` override — same override technique as `About.astro`'s `.about-photo`. Uses the `"rounded"` `PlaceholderImage` variant as a base only because it doesn't force an aspect ratio (the override below strips its border-radius anyway) — don't confuse this with an actual rounded-corner treatment. No hover-lift or idle-sway animation on the photo anymore (both removed); it's a static full-height panel, not an animated card.
- The photo is faded on its left edge via `mask-image: linear-gradient(to left, black 55%, transparent 100%)` so it blends into the hero's teal background instead of a hard seam. The mask is dropped on mobile (`max-width: 800px`, where the layout stacks the photo below the copy at a fixed `aspect-ratio: 4/5` instead of stretching full-height). `hero.image`'s placeholder default now points at `/images/lara_about.jpg` (same photo as `About.astro`, just as a stand-in until a dedicated hero photo is uploaded) — still a normal admin-editable field, no code change needed to swap it.
- **No more bottom wave divider.** The `.hero-wave` SVG (the curved teal→cream transition into the next section) was removed outright — the boundary between Hero and whatever section follows is now a flat, straight edge, matching the reference mockup. Don't resurrect the wave without discussing it first.
- `content.socialLinks` (still just `[instagram, email]`) sits as two `position: absolute` circular buttons (3.5rem, up from an earlier 2.75rem — bigger per feedback) pinned top-right of the section (a sibling of `.hero-inner`, not inside `.hero-copy`, so it overlays the photo rather than the text column), offset below the fixed navbar via `top: calc(var(--navbar-height) + ...)`. **Icon color is per-platform, not a single shared token**: each `<a>` gets `data-platform={social.platform}`, and CSS keys off that attribute — `email` → `--color-primary`, `instagram` → `--color-tertiary` — on a white circle by default. On hover the two colors swap ends: the circle's *background* becomes that platform's color and the icon turns white (`.social-links a[data-platform="X"]:hover { background: var(--color-X); color: white }`). If a 3rd platform is ever added here, it needs its own `[data-platform="..."]` color rule (idle + hover) — there's no generic fallback color for this specific spot, unlike `SocialIcon`'s own internal fallback.
- The CTA button's copy ("Let's learn together →") is hardcoded in the component, same as the previous "Book a first lesson" — it was never a `siteContent.json` field either time, so this isn't a new gap. `.hero-cta` needs its own `align-self: flex-start` — `.hero-copy` is a flex column with default `align-items: stretch`, so without this override the button (despite `.button`'s own `display: inline-flex`) stretches to the full column width instead of shrinking to fit its text. Padding is also tightened (`0.65rem 1.5rem` vs the shared `.button` class's `0.9rem 1.9rem`) for a visibly shorter pill, per feedback that the default was too tall/wide for this specific CTA.

**Typography: `--font-display` and `--font-script` are two new tokens.** `src/styles/global.css` adds `--font-display: "Bebas Neue", "Haettenschweiler", Impact, sans-serif` (a tall condensed display face — Bebas Neue is the closest free/Fontsource-available lookalike for Haettenschweiler, which isn't distributable) and `--font-script: "Beau Rivage", "Freestyle Script", cursive` (a fineline script — Beau Rivage is the closest free/Fontsource-available lookalike for Freestyle Script, a proprietary Microsoft font). Both are `@import`ed in `global.css` from their own `@fontsource` packages (`@fontsource/bebas-neue`, `@fontsource/beau-rivage`, weight 400 only — neither font ships other weights). These are distinct from `--font-heading` (Fraunces, still used by every other section's headings) and `--font-body` (Nunito). `--font-display` started Hero/Navbar-only but now also drives Pain Points', Method's, HowITeach's, and LessonsDifferent's `<h2>` headings (confirmed with Alessandro — see "Pain Points" below and the Method/HowITeach/LessonsDifferent section above); `--font-script` remains Hero/Navbar-only. Don't extend either further without discussing it first, since they were introduced for specific mockup-matching passes, not as a site-wide typographic shift.

### Pain Points: photo-left layout, icon list instead of bubbles

`PainPoints.astro` was redesigned to match `Ideas/Mockups/Painpoints.jpg`. No content-model or admin-editor change was needed — `PainPointsContent` already had everything (`heading`/`highlightWords`/`image`/`questions`/`cta`/`visible`) the new layout needs.

- **Layout swapped**: the photo (`.pain-points-portrait`) is now the left grid column, copy (heading/questions/CTA) the right — previously the reverse. `.pain-points-inner`'s grid-template-columns is `minmax(0, 0.85fr) minmax(0, 1.15fr)`, and the `minmax(0, ...)` is load-bearing, not decorative: plain `fr` units alone let a long heading or button force that track wider than its share and overflow the viewport — the same class of CSS Grid footgun already documented for Navbar's `grid-row` issue above, but via intrinsic min-width rather than auto-placement.
- **New `PlaceholderImage` variant, `"pill"`**: `border-radius: 999px; aspect-ratio: 3/4`, always cropped via `object-fit: cover`. Unlike `"portrait"` (which deliberately lets real photos keep their natural ratio uncropped, via an `img.placeholder-image.portrait` override), `"pill"` has no such exemption — a capsule shape only reads correctly if the photo is force-cropped to it.
- **Heading** now uses `--font-display` (Bebas Neue) + uppercase, the same treatment as Hero's `<h1>` — see the typography note above for why this needed sign-off. The old two-color alternation between highlighted words (`tertiary`/`secondary` alternating) is gone; every `.highlight` word is now a single `var(--color-secondary)`, matching the mockup's one consistent highlight color.
- **Questions list redesigned**: the old `.bubbles`/`.bubble` speech-bubble-with-tail treatment (bordered white cards, rotated, CSS-triangle tail) is gone entirely, replaced by `.pain-list` — plain flex rows (`.pain-item`), each a `.pain-icon-circle` (fixed size, `background: var(--color-primary-light)`) holding an inline white-stroke SVG, followed by the question text. The 4 icons (book, suitcase, speech bubble, heart) live in a small fixed `questionIcons` array in the frontmatter, indexed positionally by `i % questionIcons.length` — the same positional-array convention the file previously used for bubble `rotations` (now removed). This is deliberately **not** a new shared icon component like `SocialIcon`/`NavIcon`: these 4 icons are fixed decorative positions tied to a fixed 4-item list, not keyed by admin-editable data the way platform/section icons are.

### Italy motifs: `italy.svg` (outline) and `italy_stamp.svg` (stamp) — same mask-recolor trick, two assets

`public/images/italy.svg` (a solid white-filled Italy silhouette, not a thin outline) is rendered two different ways because its fill color is baked into the SVG's own `<path style>` (not `currentColor`), so it can't be recolored with a simple CSS `color`/`fill` override:

- `Contact.astro`: `.contact-italy`, a plain `<img>`, sized to the file's real ~0.83:1 aspect ratio (`14.5rem × 17.4rem`) — stays white, no recolor needed there.
- `Hero.astro`: `.hero-italy` is **not** an `<img>` — it's an empty `<span>` with `background-color: var(--color-secondary-light)` and `mask-image: url(...)` pointing at the same SVG. The mask lets the *shape* come from the file while the *color* comes from a live brand token, so it tints correctly when the admin changes `secondaryColor`. ~12rem wide (sized up twice from an initial too-small/too-corner first pass, per feedback), opacity 0.5, positioned via `left: 3rem; top: calc(var(--navbar-height) + 10rem)` — a decorative flourish sitting behind/left of the hero title, absolutely positioned within `.hero-copy` (re-centered via `left: 50%; translate: -50% 0` on mobile, ≤800px, top offset unchanged). This is a deliberate compromise: the reference mockup shows a thin stroke-only outline, but no such asset exists yet, so this reuses the existing solid silhouette shrunk down and tinted rather than blocking on a new SVG. If a true thin-line outline asset is ever provided, swap this back to a plain masked `<img>` or keep the mask trick (masks work identically on outline art).

`HeroContent.backgroundImage` / `--hero-bg-image` (the old configurable hero background photo, including its admin editor field) was removed entirely — Hero's background is now just the solid gradient plus this silhouette flourish.

`public/images/italy_stamp.svg` (a "Made in Italy" stamp doodle, ~1.038:1 from its 45.9mm×44.2mm viewBox, own fill hardcoded the same way) is a second, unrelated asset using the identical mask-recolor technique — see `PainPoints.astro`'s `.pain-points-stamp` below. Don't conflate the two files: `italy.svg` is the outline silhouette (Hero/Contact), `italy_stamp.svg` is the stamp graphic (Pain Points only, so far).

Two more stamp-style assets followed the same convention, each its own unrelated doodle (not variants of `italy_stamp.svg`), each used in exactly one section so far: `public/images/spritz_stamp.svg` (`HowITeach.astro`, tinted `--color-white`) and `public/images/heart_stamp.svg`/`public/images/star_stamp.svg` (`Services.astro`, both tinted `--color-tertiary` — see the Services paragraph above). If a 5th stamp is ever added, keep it as its own file/asset rather than trying to parameterize one SVG into multiple shapes — none of these share a viewBox or path structure.

### The 3-color brand system — read this before touching any color

The entire palette is derived from exactly 3 admin-editable tokens. **Never hardcode a 4th brand color or a raw hex value in a component** — if you need a new tone, add it as a `color-mix()` derived token in `:root` (`src/styles/global.css`) so it inherits admin edits automatically.

- Base tokens: `--color-primary`, `--color-secondary`, `--color-tertiary` (plus the fixed `--color-white`). Everything else is derived once in `:root`, e.g. `--color-primary-dark` (80% primary/black), `--color-primary-light` (35% primary/white), `--color-secondary-light` (65% secondary/white — used for accents that used to be "yellow": Hero's title accent line/Italy flourish, Method's subtitle/steps-label), `--color-cream` (6% **secondary**/white — deliberately derived from secondary, not primary), `--color-ink` (40% primary/black — body text is deliberately primary-tinted, not neutral grey), `--color-ink-soft` (70% primary/black). `--shadow-soft`/`--shadow-lift` are also `color-mix()` expressions so shadows track the live palette instead of being hardcoded `rgb()`.
- The 3 base values live in content, not CSS: `SiteMeta` (`src/types/content.ts`) has `primaryColor`/`secondaryColor`/`tertiaryColor`, seeded in `content/siteContent.json`. `src/layouts/Layout.astro` injects them as real custom properties via an inline `style` attribute on `<html>` (`--color-primary:${meta.primaryColor}; ...`) — inline style always wins the cascade, so this overrides the `:root` defaults, and every derived token recomputes automatically since they all reference these same var names. `<meta name="theme-color">` reads `primaryColor` directly. There used to be a separate `meta.themeColor` field for this exact purpose — it drifted into an orphaned, type-less JSON key (never read by any component) and was removed; `primaryColor` has done this job directly all along.
- The admin editor's Site Info fieldset exposes 3 `<input type="color">` pickers (`meta.primaryColor`/`secondaryColor`/`tertiaryColor`) — required no new JS, since scalar fields already go through the generic populate/read path. They have their own CSS rule (`.panel input[type="color"]`) with a fixed `width: 4.5rem; height: 3rem` box, separate from the generic text-input rule — sharing that rule (`width:100%; padding:0.6rem 0.8rem`) used to squash the native swatch down to nearly invisible.
- Every component was mechanically renamed from the old ~10-color palette (`--color-teal`, `--color-yellow`, `--color-ocher`, `--color-red`, etc.) to the new var names. `CustomCursor.astro`'s ice/orange-slice fallback illustrations got the same var renames but are decorative and exempt from strict brand-contrast rules. Actual multi-color illustration image files (cursor sprites, hero brand mark) were left untouched — they keep their natural hues regardless of the brand tokens.

### Per-section visibility toggles, and Results' title field

`PainPointsContent`, `MethodContent`, `HowITeachContent`, `LessonsDifferentContent`, `ServicesContent`, `ResultsContent`, `TestimonialsContent`, and `AboutContent` (`src/types/content.ts`) each carry a `visible: boolean` field, seeded `true` in `content/siteContent.json`. `index.astro` wraps each of those 8 section invocations in `{content.X.visible && <X content={content.X} />}` — Testimonials keeps its separate pre-existing `items.length > 0` guard on top of this, the two checks compose fine. Hero and Contact deliberately have no toggle — they're the page's structural start/end and are never meant to be hidden. Each admin fieldset got a matching `<label class="checkbox-label"><input type="checkbox" data-field="X.visible" /> ...</label>`.

Adding the checkbox required one small generic addition to the admin's `[data-field]` scalar-field JS (`populateScalarFields`/`readScalarFields`, driven by `getPath`/`setPath`): a branch checking `el.type === "checkbox"` to read/write `.checked` instead of `.value`. This is the only new JS logic the whole feature needed — every other new field, including the checkboxes' labels and Results' new title input, reuses the existing generic path unmodified.

Results was the only section with no editable title at all — it had a hardcoded `<h2>Results</h2>`. (Hero's heading comes from "Site name" and About's from "Name", both already editable, so those were intentionally left alone.) `ResultsContent` gained a `title: string`, rendered as `<h2>{content.title}</h2>` in `Results.astro`, with a new "Heading" text input (`data-field="results.title"`) in the admin's Results fieldset.
